# Digital-Security-Ops-Mastery

Practical guides for secure, privacy-conscious smartphone and PC setups—from Android backups and Nextcloud to Linux automation.

# Topics

## ![app_image](/docs/_static/ico/nextcloud.ico) [Nextcloud](docs/nextcloud/README_en.md)

Guidance for setting up and synchronizing files, contacts, calendars, and notes with self-hosted or managed Nextcloud.

## ![app_image](/docs/_static/ico/android.ico) [De-Googled-phone](docs/de-googled-phone/README_en.md)

Compare alternative Android operating systems and privacy-conscious apps. Device support and security properties vary by operating system.

## ![app_image](/docs/_static/ico/adaway.ico) [Child-proof phone](docs/child-proof-phone/README_en.md)

The guide covers screen-time limits, app locks, content filters, and device location. These measures do not replace supervision or provide complete parental controls.

## ![app_image](/docs/_static/ico/backup.ico) [Backup and restore phone](docs/backup-restore-phone/README.md)

The backup overview is currently available in German and covers Android passwords, messages, photos, other data, and restore limitations.

## ![app_image](/docs/_static/ico/ubuntu.ico) [Linux (Ubuntu/Kubuntu) automations](docs/ubuntu-linux-automations/README.md)

Guides and scripts for Kubuntu, NAS mounts, encrypted synchronization, and image and video processing.
