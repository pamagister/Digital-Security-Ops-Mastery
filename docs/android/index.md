## 🧭 What to expect

- **No guide is a complete security boundary.** Android versions, device manufacturers, and app permissions affect what can be enforced.
- **Backup is not synchronization.** A synchronized folder can propagate deletion or corruption; keep a separate, tested backup.
- **Check official instructions before flashing a device.** Unlocking a bootloader can erase data and may permanently reduce device functionality.
- **Parental controls are not supervision.** App locks and screen-time limits can be bypassed depending on the device and Android version.
